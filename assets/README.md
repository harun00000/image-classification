# Page background

Place a JPEG named `cat-background.jpg` in this folder. The app embeds this local
file as a full-page background; no external image service is used.

Choose a realistic photo or high-quality realistic artwork of a black or dark
gray cat on the right, with dark empty space on the left. A landscape image around
2400 × 1600 pixels works well. Leave some space around the cat: the background is
cropped to fill the screen, so its edges may fall outside the viewport.

Use an image you can publish and include any required attribution here. Compress
the JPEG to roughly 1–2 MB to keep page loads quick.

The app adds dark gradients for readability and stronger dimming on small
screens. Without this file, it uses a dark background. After adding or replacing
the image, rerun the app to see the change.
